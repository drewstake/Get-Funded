CONFIRMED CAUSE
The chart showed actual BX executions from the same running exchange. The old
matcher applied the fair-value guard to a player's resting quote but exempted
a bot's quote. It skipped the better TP while executing a worse bot ask.

First observed bypass: trade 23447624, step 8709491, simulation time 2177372.75.

BX's 0.25 tick and existing 20-tick guard allow an incoming buy to pay at most
fair + 5.00, and an incoming sell to receive at least fair - 5.00. Both the TP
and worse bot ask were outside that buy-side guard. The fix blocks both
consistently; it does not grant retroactive fills or invent 7,946 contracts of
liquidity. Buy-side collar eligibility for that TP alone requires fair >=
5,229.75; opposing quantity and all other restrictions still apply.

Historical tape outside the observation lacks the pre-match fair value and
order identities needed to attribute every old candle. Those records are
preserved without assigning unsupported causes. Client packet samples and the
chart's data path confirm that BX candles originate from this engine's trades.

IMPLEMENTED RULES AND RELIABILITY
* Best eligible price, then FIFO, independent of participant type. The same
  directional execution guard prevents bypassing a blocked price with a worse
  one. Immediate out-of-band limits reject explicitly for players and bots.
* Existing absolute entry bands, player entry allowances, capital, risk
  parameters and strategy settings are preserved. Existing participant risk
  models remain; the execution-price rule is shared.
* Dynamic blocked status, bounded order status history, and the last 64 exact
  submission-rejection reasons. New trades include both order IDs/priorities,
  aggressor direction and fair value. Studio's Execution diagnostic exposes
  rules, raw order levels, current reasons, recent fills and queue depth.
* Every fill rechecks quantity, eligibility/margin, position identity and
  reduce-only limits. Matches settle in chunks; self-trade prevention cancels
  the older own quote without creating an execution.
* TP quantity tracks the remaining position; TP/SL/manual exits remove obsolete
  siblings and cannot reverse a closed or replaced position. A blocked bot
  target retains one pending order and recovers without repeated creation.
* Same-price quantity reduction and no-op amendments retain priority. Repricing
  or increasing remaining quantity loses priority. Expected state and capacity
  are checked before changes. Original = filled + remaining + cancelled.
* Accepted requests enter FIFO batches of at most 16 before each atomic step;
  the tail remains queued. Capacity is 256; overflow receives a busy response.
  The existing 8 ms simulation budget and carry-forward backlog are retained.
* Persisted request sessions, consumed-ID watermarks and 128 cached replies
  prevent duplicate mutations after retry, reply eviction or old-session replay.
  The client resumes above the server's accepted-ID watermark. A new deliberate
  request is a new operation; reconnect does not replay an old request.
* Both parties and tape settle before close callbacks. Callback exceptions are
  recorded and cannot leave half a fill settled.
* Checkpoint V16 persists the journal and diagnostics with account/order state,
  reads V1–V16 and fences older writers. Public Capture remains a detached copy.

REMAINING LIMITATIONS
Saving is still synchronous (~66 ms here), and one atomic step can exceed the
8 ms budget. Batches/backlog are bounded; fills cannot be safely preempted.
Checkpoint durability is not a per-fill event log: an abrupt crash can lose
changes after the last successful checkpoint, normally up to 15 real seconds.
Old executables cannot open V16. Do not restore the investigation snapshot over
subsequent real trading. There is no guarantee of liquidity or future fills.
