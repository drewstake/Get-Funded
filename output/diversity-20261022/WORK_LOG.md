## One-sided quotes and robust rolling allocations

The corrected rolling experiments exposed a pre-existing Whale quote-selection bug. Lua's `and/or` selector falls back when the intended side is nil. An aggressive sell with no bid could reach arithmetic on nil; a buy could incorrectly act on the other side. The installed baseline reproduces three failures in four isolated one-sided cases. `one-sided-whale-baseline-reproduction.json` preserves the evidence. Explicit nil-preserving conditional selectors fix all four in the new frozen derivatives; the matching engine, accounting and safeguards are unchanged. The two `*-safe` research arms remain aborted with partial logs, never reported as complete aggregate comparisons. The first two aborted rolling versions also remain preserved.

`rolling-portfolio-robust` and `rolling-patient-robust` complete all six development cases. Their mean 30-minute ranges rise to .824% and 1.201%, but they fail long-leg occupancy, spread, missing quotes and recovery. Recovery transfers rise to $12.319m and $31.472m versus $5.628m baseline. Current BX movement is less concentrated in a few isolated jumps, but this does not justify the liquidity/funding cost. The separate `inner-patient-quote-safe` control has no rolling allocations; it passes numerical gates but still has quiet waves and no strict current-BX pullback. All three current-world migration probes pass. No native release verification has run.

## Round 15: allocation execution cost and touch participation

Missing quotes in the original-depth rolling run concentrate in BT. Inspection finds that rolling risk entry has no spread or available-depth check, unlike Stretch signal entry. This is an observed omission, not proof of causation. Two frozen arms therefore start from `rolling-portfolio-depth`: `rolling-cost-aware` waits if spread plus the existing three-tick slippage allowance exceeds 25% of the existing stop distance; `rolling-touch-participation` adds a child-size cap of 25% of actual opposite best-level quantity, rounded down. Both require two-sided quotes for new risk. Risk reductions, stops and manual protections are unaffected by these new discretionary-entry guards. No capital or liquidity is manufactured, no price target is introduced, and target directions/timing remain independent. Dedicated tests cover wide/missing quotes, retained risk exits and real-depth limits. Full unchanged development gates and candle review still apply. Separate validation remains untouched.

Both final arms completed all six cases. Cost-aware entry reduced recovery to $3.239m and mean p95 spread to 4.556 ticks, but failed long-leg occupancy (76.11% versus maximum 74.15%) and quote availability (.1034% missing versus .1000% maximum). Current BX has three strict pullbacks and .5242% minute RV, but this does not override the two full-batch failures. Touch participation fixes quote availability (.0787%) and lowers recovery to $2.959m, while failing occupancy (79.54%) and current BX movement (.2574% versus .4304% floor). Neither is promoted.
